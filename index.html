How it works

  1. Each morning, a scheduled job collects new postings from company career boards on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday.
  2. Your experience is compared against every posting in your time window, and the closest ones are shortlisted.
  3. Claude scores each shortlisted role from 1 (no match) to 5 (strong match) and explains the score in one line.
  4. Anything below 4 is removed.

Coverage is limited to the employers in this site's list. See sources for which boards were reached today.
